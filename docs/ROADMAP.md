## 🗺️ 21-Day Daily Quest Ladder

### 🍁 Week 1: Prefix Sums & Difference Arrays (Days 01–07)
*Objective: Master 1D and 2D cumulative transformations to answer range queries and batch updates in $O(1)$.*

- **Day 01 (Oct 26):** [Kuriyama Mirai's Stones](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/433/B) (Rating: 1200)
  - *Topic:* 1D Prefix Sums on original vs. sorted arrays.
  - *Reading:* `CPH.pdf` — Chapter 9 (Range Queries, Section 9.1).
- **Day 02 (Oct 27):** [Ilya and Queries](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/313/B) (Rating: 1100)
  - *Topic:* Boolean indicator prefix arrays.
- **Day 03 (Oct 28):** [Greg and Array](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/296/C) (Rating: 1400)
  - *Topic:* Two-stage Difference Arrays.
- **Day 04 (Oct 29):** [Little Girl and Maximum Sum](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/276/C) (Rating: 1500)
  - *Topic:* Difference Array frequency counting + Greedy rearrangement.
- **Day 05 (Oct 30):** [Subarray Divisibility](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1398/C) (Rating: 1300)
  - *Topic:* Prefix Sums with Hash Maps / Remainder Counting.
- **Day 06 (Oct 31):** [Karen and Coffee](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/816/B) (Rating: 1400)
  - *Topic:* Difference Array over coordinate ranges up to $2 \times 10^5$.
- **Day 07 (Nov 01):** [Good Subarrays](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1398/C) (Rating: 1300)
  - *Topic:* Algebraic index shifting: $(pref[r] - r = pref[l-1] - (l-1))$.

---

### 🍁 Week 2: Constructive Algorithms & Invariants (Days 08–14)
*Objective: Develop combinatorial intuition, analyze small cases, find parity invariants, and handle extreme elements.*

- **Day 08 (Nov 02):** [Make It Good](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1385/C) (Rating: 1200)
  - *Topic:* Right-to-left monotonic prefix/suffix construction.
- **Day 09 (Nov 03):** [Even Subset Sum Problem](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1323/A) (Rating: 800)
  - *Topic:* Parity invariants (odd + odd = even).
- **Day 10 (Nov 04):** [Array Cancellation](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1405/B) (Rating: 1000)
  - *Topic:* Running balance invariants.
- **Day 11 (Nov 05):** [Permutation Minimization by Deque](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1579/E1) (Rating: 1000)
  - *Topic:* Greedy deque construction for lexicographical optimality.
- **Day 12 (Nov 06):** [Non-Zero Segments](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1426/D) (Rating: 1300)
  - *Topic:* Preventing prefix collision with greedy infinity insertions.
- **Day 13 (Nov 07):** [Omkar and Baseball](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1372/B) (Rating: 1100)
  - *Topic:* Derangement cycles and extremal sorting moves.
- **Day 14 (Nov 08):** [Equalize the Array](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1490/F) (Rating: 1300)
  - *Topic:* Frequency compression and candidate sweeping.

---

### 🍁 Week 3: Greedy Strategies & Number Theory (Days 15–21)
*Objective: Prove exchange arguments, optimal substructure, and apply modular arithmetic with Fermat's Little Theorem.*

- **Day 15 (Nov 09):** [Two Teams Composing](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1335/C) (Rating: 1100)
  - *Topic:* Pigeonhole principle & frequency bounds.
- **Day 16 (Nov 10):** [BerSU Ball](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/489/B) (Rating: 1200)
  - *Topic:* Two pointers on sorted sequences.
- **Day 17 (Nov 11):** [Maximum Product Strikes Back](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1660/D) (Rating: 1600)
  - *Topic:* Sign parity tracking and greedy zero-segment partitioning.
- **Day 18 (Nov 12):** [Product of Three Numbers](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1294/C) (Rating: 1300)
  - *Topic:* Prime factorization and minimal distinct divisor extraction.
- **Day 19 (Nov 13):** [Omkar and Last Class of Math](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1372/B) (Rating: 1300)
  - *Topic:* Minimizing LCM via the smallest prime factor $p$.
- **Day 20 (Nov 14):** [Same Parity Summands](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1352/B) (Rating: 1200)
  - *Topic:* Constructive partition with uniform parity.
- **Day 21 (Nov 15):** [K-th Not Divisible by n](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1352/C) (Rating: 1200)
  - *Topic:* Quotient-remainder mapping in $O(1)$.
